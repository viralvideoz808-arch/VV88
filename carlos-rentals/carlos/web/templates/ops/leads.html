Saw someone asking for a car in a Facebook group, on Marketplace or anywhere else? Paste it here — the Qualifier scores it and you'll get a suggested reply to send yourself.

How leads are found

  • Threads: keyword search of public posts → one public reply.
  • Instagram & Facebook: comments on our own posts → one private reply (Meta's rule), then the DM conversation.
  • Instagram hashtags: Meta hides the author, so these are "manual" — open the post and engage yourself.
  • No profile scraping, no cold DMs, opt-outs respected.
