Renting

What do I need to rent a car?

A valid driver's license, a credit or debit card in the driver's name for the refundable security deposit, and proof of insurance (or add our collision damage waiver). International visitors: bring your home license and passport.

How old do I have to be?

Drivers must be {{ settings['rental.min_age'] }} or older. Drivers under {{ settings['rental.young_driver_age'] }} pay a young driver fee of {{ settings['rental.young_driver_fee']|money }} per day.

Can I pay with a debit card?

Yes. The deposit is held on the card and released after the car is returned in the same condition.

How is the price calculated?

Daily rate × days, or the weekly rate for each full week when that's cheaper. Extras, any young driver fee, and {{ (settings['rental.tax_rate'] * 100)|round(2) }}% tax are added — the booking form shows the full total before you request.

Can you deliver the car?

Yes — choose "{{ ex.delivery.label if ex.delivery else 'Delivery' }}" when booking{% if ex.delivery and ex.delivery.flat %} ({{ ex.delivery.flat|money }}){% endif %} and enter your address.

What's your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation more than 24 hours before pickup — use Manage booking. Inside 24 hours, please call us.

Fuel, tolls and mileage?

Return the car with the same fuel or charge level. Tolls and tickets during your rental are your responsibility. Normal mileage is included; ask us about long road trips.

Buying & selling

Can I test drive a car?

Of course — request a time on the car's page, and bring your license.

Do you take trade-ins?

Yes. Get an offer on the sell / trade-in page and put it toward any car on our lot.

Do you offer financing?

Send a financing request — no credit check and no SSN on our site — and we'll follow up with options.

Messages

Who answers the chat and DMs?

An automated assistant answers right away; a team member reviews conversations and takes over whenever needed. Reply STOP any time and we won't message you again.
