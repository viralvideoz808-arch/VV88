Logo

The mark &
lockups

An M whose outer strokes rise into bull horns: building (M), markets (the bull) and upward momentum in one shape.

Clear space

Keep empty space around the logo equal to the height of one horn (¼ of the mark).

Minimum size

Mark: 24 px on screen / 8 mm in print. Horizontal logo: 120 px / 30 mm wide.

Mascot

The MKB
Bull

A low-poly bull head in jungle green, built as a real 3D model and drawn live. It follows the viewer's cursor and changes mood with the "market": higher means bullish, lower means bearish.

  • — Use it as the hero of the website, intros and video end-cards.
  • — Always on black or dark green, never on busy photos.
  • — The logo stays the signature; the bull is the personality.
