    The mascot

    Meet the
    MKB Bull

    A bull is the market's symbol for confidence and growth: horns up, moving forward. Mine is built from triangles like a 3D model, rendered live in your browser, and it watches what you do. It stands for the three things MKB is about: building with precision, trading with discipline and growing with partners.

    Move your cursor up and it turns bullish. Move it down and it frowns. Click it and it snorts.
