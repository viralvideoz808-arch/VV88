# MKB brand guidelines

**Moses Bright Kang'ara · MKB** · *Build. Trade. Scale.*
Logo: **Candle M** · Theme: **Trading Terminal** (chosen from the options in `brand-options/`).

## Essence
- **Promise:** technology that works and knowledge that's honest.
- **Personality:** disciplined · sharp · honest · generous.
- **Audience:** business owners who need websites and apps, new and growing traders, founders with ideas worth building.
- **Three pillars:** **Build** (IT services) · **Trade** (Forex, Binary, Crypto) · **Scale** (partnerships and business growth).

## Logo
The mark is an **M drawn as five candlesticks**, read like a price chart. The tall bullish outer candles carry the shape. The hollow bearish candle in the centre is the dip every trader learns to manage.

- **Clear space:** the width of two candles (¼ of the mark) on every side.
- **Minimum size:** mark 24 px / 8 mm; horizontal logo 120 px / 30 mm wide.
- **Don't** stretch, rotate, recolour, add effects or place on low-contrast backgrounds. On light backgrounds use the `-light` versions.

## Typography (both free on Google Fonts)
- **Space Grotesk Bold**, uppercase with tight tracking: headlines, the MKB wordmark, big numbers. Space Grotesk Medium for long reading (lessons).
- **JetBrains Mono**: body text, buttons, labels, prices and codes. It's the terminal voice of the brand.

## Shapes
Square edges: 8 px corners on cards, 4 px on buttons and tags. Thin 1 px green lines and a faint grid in the background, like a trading terminal.

## Mascot: the MKB Bull
A low-poly 3D bull head in jungle green, rendered live in the browser. It follows the viewer's cursor, turns bullish when the cursor rises and bearish when it falls, and snorts when clicked. Use it for the website hero, social posts and video end-cards. The Candle M stays the signature; the bull is the personality.

## Voice
- **Clear over clever:** "A website that takes M-Pesa payments, ready in two weeks."
- **Confident, not hype:** back claims with numbers. Never promise guaranteed profits.
- **Honest about risk:** every trading message carries the risk.
- **Warm and direct:** talk like a partner, not a corporation.
