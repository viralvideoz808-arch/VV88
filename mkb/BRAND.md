# MKB brand guidelines

**Moses Bright Kang'ara · MKB** · *Build. Trade. Scale.*

## Essence
- **Promise:** technology that works and knowledge that's honest.
- **Personality:** disciplined · bold · honest · generous.
- **Audience:** business owners who need websites and apps, new and growing traders, founders with ideas worth building.
- **Three pillars:** **Build** (IT services) · **Trade** (Forex, Binary, Crypto) · **Scale** (partnerships and business growth).

## Logo
The mark is an **M whose outer strokes rise into bull horns**: building (M), markets (the bull) and upward momentum in one shape.

| Asset | File |
|---|---|
| Badge mark (app icon, avatar) | `public/assets/brand/mkb-mark.svg` |
| Horizontal lockup, dark / light / white | `mkb-logo-horizontal.svg`, `-light.svg`, `-white.svg` |
| Stacked lockup with tagline | `mkb-logo-stacked.svg`, `-light.svg` |
| One-colour marks | `mkb-mark-white.svg`, `-black.svg`, `-jungle.svg` |
| Favicon | `favicon.svg` |

- **Clear space:** the height of one horn (¼ of the mark) on every side.
- **Minimum size:** mark 24 px / 8 mm; horizontal logo 120 px / 30 mm wide.
- **Don't** stretch, rotate, recolour, add effects or place on low-contrast backgrounds.

## Typography (all free on Google Fonts)
- **Syne Extra Bold**, uppercase with tight tracking: headlines, wordmark, big numbers.
- **Manrope**: body text (Medium) and buttons/labels (Extra Bold).
- **JetBrains Mono**: prices, codes, small uppercase labels.

## Mascot: the MKB Bull
A low-poly 3D bull head in jungle green, rendered live in the browser. It follows the viewer's cursor, turns bullish when the cursor rises and bearish when it falls, and snorts when clicked. Use it for the website hero, social posts, intros and video end-cards. Keep it on black or dark green.

## Voice
- **Clear over clever:** "A website that takes M-Pesa payments, ready in two weeks."
- **Confident, not hype:** back claims with numbers. Never promise guaranteed profits.
- **Honest about risk:** every trading message carries the risk.
- **Warm and direct:** talk like a partner, not a corporation.
